## Full description
The brainrots are coming! Pizza snails, grumpy toasters, chili peppers on wheels and cheese butterflies are rushing your castle. Build the craziest defense ever.

**A REAL TOWER DEFENSE, MADE FOR MOBILE**
• Play one-handed, in portrait, a few minutes per level.
• Tap a spot, pick a turret, upgrade it: everything stays clear and readable, even in the heat of battle.

**12 TURRETS, CHOICES THAT MATTER**
• Rifle, SMG, shotgun, sniper, rockets, minigun, cryo, laser, tesla, plasma, drone and mines.
• At level 3, pick a specialization: burst fire or tracer rounds, cluster blast or homing missile…
• Reach Legendary level and unlock spectacular effects.

**80 LEVELS, 8 WORLDS, 8 GIANT BOSSES**
• Meadow, desert, ice field, volcano, all the way to futuristic worlds.
• Every world ends with a huge boss with its own powers.
• Varied enemies: flyers, armored, invisible, healers, jumpers, shielded robots…

**GROW YOUR CASTLE**
• Walls, repairs, treasury, cannon: watch your castle grow from a wooden fort to a golden palace.
• Unlock abilities: air strike, blizzard, gold rain.

**PROGRESSION AND SURPRISES**
• Talent tree with ULTIMATE talents.
• Chests to open, boosts to use at the right time, skins for your turrets.
• Daily quests, lucky wheel, season pass, Heroic challenges and endless mode.
• Rebirth: start over stronger, against mutated brainrots.

**FAIR AND STRESS-FREE**
• No purchase is needed to finish the game.
• Ads are always optional: you choose to watch them for a bonus. Never a forced ad.
• Play offline, your progress stays on your device.

Defend your castle. Stop the brainrots. Become a legend!
